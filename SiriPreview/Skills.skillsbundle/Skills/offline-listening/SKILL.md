---
name: offline-listening
description: Prepare music or audiobooks from the current Jellyfin profile for offline listening on this device.
---
Find Library Items locates a named book or album. Use Get Contents of Library Item when someone requests tracks from an album. Ask the person to choose between editions if the title and artist or author are ambiguous.

Pass the selected items to Manage Download when the person asks to save them on this device. A queued transfer still needs time and available storage. Use Open Downloads to review progress. Resume Audiobook continues listening from the current profile's saved progress.
